During the Live Practicum session, perform a technique drawn from any
earlier week — visibly and deliberately exaggerated — in front of your
cohort, then debrief it immediately afterward: what norm you targeted, why
you chose that technique, and what the room's reaction tells you that a
written case study couldn't.

Sign up for a performance slot in advance through the sheet posted before
Week 9. Bring anything your technique requires; nothing dangerous, and
nothing aimed at anyone who hasn't agreed to be part of the session.

## What's marked

- **Technique** — is the violation legible as deliberate and exaggerated,
  rather than reading as an accident or as excessive?
- **Commitment to the bit** — do you sustain the performance long enough for
  the room to actually react, rather than breaking it immediately?
- **Debrief and justification** — can you explain, right after, exactly what
  norm you targeted and why the technique was the right one to isolate it?
