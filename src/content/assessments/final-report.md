Choose one norm this course studied — it doesn't have to be the one from
your Case Study — and write a single analytical report making a specific
claim about how that norm is actually enforced, and exactly what it takes to
break it visibly. Use your Case Study and your Practicum Demonstration as
evidence; you may also draw on any other week's material.

1500 to 2500 words. This is not a diary of the semester: it's an argument,
and everything you include should be doing work for that argument.

## What's marked

There's no fixed weighting here — this is assessed holistically, as one
piece of writing. The description above is what we're actually looking for:
a specific, defensible claim; evidence you gathered yourself rather than
material restated from lectures; and a structure organised around the claim
rather than around the order the course was taught in.
