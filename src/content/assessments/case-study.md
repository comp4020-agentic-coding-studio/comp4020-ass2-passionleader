Pick one norm from Weeks 2 through 4 (coughing and sneezing, elevator power
dynamics, or library noise) and write it up as a case study: the norm as it
actually exists, the smallest deliberate act you observed breaking it, and a
precise account of what happened next.

This must be built from something you actually observed, not something you
imagine would happen. Two to three pages, following the method set out in
Week 1: observe, isolate, describe.

## What's marked

- **Clarity of the isolated act** — is the violation you're describing
  actually the smallest possible deviation from the norm, or does it bundle
  in other things?
- **Quality of observed evidence** — did you watch this happen, and can the
  write-up show that rather than assert it?
- **Analytical connection to the method** — does the write-up use the
  observe/isolate/describe method from Week 1, rather than just narrate an
  anecdote?
