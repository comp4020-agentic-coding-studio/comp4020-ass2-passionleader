## Activity

Walk a short, familiar route past at least one public bin and note where
litter actually accumulates relative to it — right at the bin, a few metres
short, or somewhere else entirely.

## Record your reaction

Note whether you can identify who dropped anything. The lecture's point is
that littering is usually audience-less — you're reading a trace, not
watching an act.

## Reflection

In your own words, explain how isolating an audience-less act changes the
method: you're reading the aftermath instead of watching the moment, and
what that changes about what counts as evidence.
