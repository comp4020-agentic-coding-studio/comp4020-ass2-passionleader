## Activity

In pairs, take the deniable-cough case from the lecture — a pointed cough
timed exactly as a classmate's bag-rustling stops — and generate one variant
where the timing, target, or aftermath signal is more ambiguous than the
original.

## Record your reaction

Note which of the lecture's three signals (timing, target, aftermath) is
doing the most work in your variant when deciding whether a room would read
the cough as accidental or performed.

## Reflection

Rewrite the deck's Bad manner example as its Good manner counterpart in your
own words, and state in one sentence why the good-manner version costs the
room less than the deniable cough does.
