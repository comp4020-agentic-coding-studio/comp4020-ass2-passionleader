The course ends where academic work usually does: writing it up. Your Final
Report is not a diary of what you did in the Practicum — it's an analytical
account of one norm, chosen from the ones this course studied, that uses your
own case studies and your Practicum as evidence for a specific claim about
how that norm actually works, what enforces it, and what it takes to break
it visibly.

This week is a working session rather than a lecture: bringing a draft claim,
testing it against the method the course opened with in Week 1, and leaving
with a report structure you can actually finish. There's no new topic here —
just the discipline of turning eleven weeks of noticing into one argument.

## Outline

- from case study to claim: what makes an argument out of a pile of
  observations
- using the Practicum as evidence, not anecdote
- structuring the report so the claim, not the chronology, does the work
- your Final Report is due this week — see the
  [assessment page](/assessments/final-report/) for what it's marked against
